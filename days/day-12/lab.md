# 🧪 Day 12 Lab — Cross-framework mapping

## Scenario
You are a GRC analyst supporting VishwaTech Bank.

## Tasks
1. Define scope.
2. Identify assets/processes.
3. Identify risks.
4. Select controls.
5. Assign owners.
6. Define evidence.
7. Test the controls.
8. Record findings.
9. Define remediation.
10. Report residual risk.

## Required output
Complete the files under `templates/`.

## Success criteria
- Business context is clear.
- Risks are written as risk statements.
- Controls address risks.
- Evidence is specific.
- Ownership is explicit.
- Findings have remediation.
- Residual risk is explained.
