# 📘 Day 15 Deep Dive — Internal Audit & CAPA

## Executive explanation
Teach audit planning, criteria, scope, risk-based sampling, evidence, findings and report writing. CAPA means corrective and preventive action; root-cause analysis prevents superficial closure. Explain issue validation and closure evidence.

## 🧠 Mental model

```text
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTEXT / ASSETS
      ↓
RISK SCENARIO
      ↓
CONTROL OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTROL ACTIVITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
FINDING / EFFECTIVE
      ↓
REMEDIATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
EXECUTIVE DECISION
```

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
