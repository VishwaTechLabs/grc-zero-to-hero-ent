# 🔐 Full Answer Key — Day 10

### Quiz answer guide
1. Define the concept in business language.
2. Connect it to a measurable business objective.
3. Identify a plausible risk scenario.
4. Select a control that directly reduces the risk.
5. Name evidence that proves operation.
6. Describe a repeatable test.
7. State what constitutes a failure.
8. Recommend remediation with an owner and date.
9. Define one meaningful KRI/KPI/KCI.
10. Explain residual risk and the required decision.

### Practical answer
Define an ISMS scope, create a mini risk treatment plan and map selected controls to evidence.

### Homework model
Prepare a readiness checklist for an organization seeking ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

**Grading rule:** Accept different answers when the student's reasoning is traceable, evidence-
based and consistent with the defined scope.
