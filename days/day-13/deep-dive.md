# 📘 Day 13 Deep Dive — SOC 2 + PCI DSS

## Executive explanation
Introduce SOC 2 as an assurance reporting context based on Trust Services Criteria and PCI DSS as a payment-card security standard. Teach scoping, evidence, control ownership, testing and remediation. Emphasize that assessment scope matters and that a single certificate/report does not prove every control everywhere.

## 🧠 Mental model

```text
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
CONTROL ACTIVITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
FINDING / EFFECTIVE
      ↓
REMEDIATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
EXECUTIVE DECISION
```

## Key vocabulary

- **evidence** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
