# 👨‍🏫 Instructor Notes — Day 21

### Suggested 3-hour flow
- 15 min recap
- 40 min theory
- 25 min visual explanation
- 60 min lab
- 20 min presentation
- 10 min quiz
- 10 min interview
- Homework

### Trainer emphasis
Teach reasoning, not memorization. Ask:
**What business risk? What control? Who owns it? What evidence? How tested? What next?**
