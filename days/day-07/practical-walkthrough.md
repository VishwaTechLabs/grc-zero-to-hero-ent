# 🧪 Complete Practical Walkthrough — Day 07

## Scenario
VishwaTech Bank operates customer-facing applications, cloud workloads, enterprise identity,
SOC capabilities, sensitive data and critical third parties.

## Task
Write ten control statements that are testable and map each to a risk.

## Step 1 — Define scope
Write the business process, systems, data, people and suppliers included.

## Step 2 — Identify risk
Use this format:

> Because **[cause]**, **[event]** could occur, resulting in **[business impact]**.

## Step 3 — Select a control
Write a control with:
- owner
- frequency
- trigger
- activity
- evidence
- exception path

## Step 4 — Define evidence
Good evidence answers **what, who, when, scope and result**.

## Step 5 — Test
Use:
- inquiry
- inspection
- observation
- reperformance
- analytics

## Step 6 — Conclude
Choose:
- Effective
- Partially effective
- Ineffective
- Not applicable

## Step 7 — Finding
Write:
**Condition → Criteria → Cause → Effect/Risk → Recommendation → Owner → Due date**

## Step 8 — Residual risk
Re-score after treatment and state whether the residual risk is within appetite.

## Expected student submission
1. Completed risk register
2. Control mapping
3. Evidence index
4. Test sheet
5. Finding/remediation
6. Executive summary
