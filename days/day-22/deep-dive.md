# 📘 Day 22 Deep Dive — Metrics, KRI/KPI/KCI & Reporting

## Executive explanation
Metrics must support decisions. KPIs measure performance, KRIs signal risk exposure and KCIs indicate control health. Teach leading vs lagging indicators, thresholds, trends, data quality, ownership and executive storytelling. Avoid dashboards that only count activity without demonstrating risk reduction.

## 🧠 Mental model

```text
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTEXT / ASSETS
      ↓
RISK SCENARIO
      ↓
CONTROL OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTROL ACTIVITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
FINDING / EFFECTIVE
      ↓
REMEDIATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
EXECUTIVE DECISION
```

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
