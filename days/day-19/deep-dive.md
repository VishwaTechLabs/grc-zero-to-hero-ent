# 📘 Day 19 Deep Dive — SOC / Incident / Vulnerability GRC

## Executive explanation
GRC translates operational security into governance evidence. Cover incident response plans, severity, escalation, notification, post-incident review, vulnerability SLAs, patch exceptions and security metrics. Teach evidence chains from alert → ticket → investigation → remediation → closure.

## 🧠 Mental model

```text
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTEXT / ASSETS
      ↓
RISK SCENARIO
      ↓
CONTROL OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTROL ACTIVITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
FINDING / EFFECTIVE
      ↓
REMEDIATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
EXECUTIVE DECISION
```

## Key vocabulary
- **incident lifecycle** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **severity** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **vulnerability SLA** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **patch exception** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **post-incident review** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
