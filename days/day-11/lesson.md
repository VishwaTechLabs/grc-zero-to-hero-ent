# Day 11 — NIST CSF/RMF/800-53

## 1. Business context
A GRC professional translates business objectives into measurable governance, risk,
controls and assurance.

## 2. Topic
CSF outcomes, RMF lifecycle, categorization, selection, assessment and monitoring.

## 3. Core model
**Objective → Risk → Control → Owner → Evidence → Test → Finding → Remediation → Residual Risk**

## 4. Concepts to teach
- Scope and business context
- Stakeholders and ownership
- Risk and control relationship
- Evidence and assurance
- Exceptions and remediation
- Metrics and executive reporting

## 5. Enterprise scenario
VishwaTech Bank operates customer applications, cloud infrastructure, corporate identity,
security operations, sensitive data and critical third parties.

## 6. Instructor demonstration
Show one complete example from business objective to evidence and executive decision.

## 7. Student activity
Complete the practical lab and present the result in 5 minutes.

## 8. Learning outcome
The learner must explain **why the control exists, what risk it addresses, who owns it,
what evidence proves operation and what happens when it fails.**
