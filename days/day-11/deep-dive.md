# 📘 Day 11 Deep Dive — NIST CSF/RMF/800-53

## Executive explanation
Teach CSF 2.0 as outcome-oriented cybersecurity risk guidance and RMF as a system-level risk-management lifecycle. Explain Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize and Monitor in the RMF. Show how controls, assessment procedures and organizational profiles fit together. Emphasize that frameworks organize risk decisions; they do not automatically make an organization compliant.

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
