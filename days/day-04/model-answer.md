# ✅ Model Answer — Day 04

## Model scenario answer

### Business objective
Protect the availability, integrity and confidentiality of the critical business service while
maintaining accountable governance and auditable evidence.

### Example risk
Because a required governance/control activity is not consistently performed, a security or
compliance failure could occur, resulting in financial, operational, legal, customer or
reputational impact.

### Example control
The designated control owner performs the defined activity at the stated frequency, records
the result in an auditable system, investigates exceptions and obtains approval for unresolved
exceptions.

### Evidence
- system-generated report
- ticket/workflow record
- approval record
- test result
- exception record
- review sign-off

### Test
Select a defined population, inspect evidence for the review period, verify completeness,
reperform selected checks where possible and document exceptions.

### Example finding
**Condition:** Required activity was not consistently evidenced.  
**Criteria:** The approved control requires the activity and retained evidence.  
**Cause:** Ownership and exception handling were unclear.  
**Effect/Risk:** The organization may not detect or prevent the underlying risk in time.  
**Recommendation:** Clarify ownership, automate evidence capture where practical and implement
exception escalation with expiry.  
**Residual risk:** Reassess after remediation.

### Why this is a good answer
It is traceable, testable, evidence-driven and communicates business impact instead of merely
naming a framework.
