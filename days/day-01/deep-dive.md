# 📘 Day 01 Deep Dive — GRC & Cybersecurity Foundations

## Executive explanation
GRC connects governance, risk and assurance to business objectives. Cybersecurity is one part of a broader information-security and enterprise-risk system. Teach CIA as a business-impact model: confidentiality protects secrecy, integrity protects correctness and trust, and availability protects business continuity. Introduce assets, processes, threats, vulnerabilities, controls, owners, evidence, issues and residual risk.

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.
