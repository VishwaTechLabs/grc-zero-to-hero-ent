# 👨‍🏫 Master Instructor Guide

## Daily rhythm
15 min recap → 40 min theory → 25 min visual → 60 min lab → 20 min presentation →
10 min quiz → 10 min interview.

## Teaching philosophy
Never teach a framework as memorization. Teach:

**Business objective → risk → control → evidence → test → finding → remediation → residual risk.**

## Trainer checklist
- [ ] Business impact
- [ ] Risk owner
- [ ] Control owner
- [ ] Evidence
- [ ] Test procedure
- [ ] Finding
- [ ] Remediation
- [ ] Residual risk
- [ ] Executive metric

## Classroom rule
Use synthetic data. Never upload confidential company evidence to a public repository.
