# 🏆 Capstone Master Answer Guide

A high-quality submission should contain **traceability**:

```text
Business Objective
  ↓
Business Service
  ↓
Asset / Data / Dependency
  ↓
Risk Scenario
  ↓
Risk Rating
  ↓
Control Objective
  ↓
Control
  ↓
Framework Mapping
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Control Test
  ↓
Finding
  ↓
CAPA
  ↓
Residual Risk
  ↓
KRI/KPI/KCI
  ↓
Executive Decision
```

## Minimum capstone portfolio
- 25 risks
- 40 controls
- 40 evidence references
- 10 control tests
- 5 findings
- 5 remediation plans
- 5 framework mappings
- 3 vendor assessments
- 1 cloud assessment
- 1 IAM assessment
- 1 AI assessment
- 1 OT/ICS assessment
- 1 BIA
- 1 executive dashboard
- 1 board presentation

## Model grading
**Excellent:** traceable, evidence-based, business-aware, testable and executive-ready.  
**Weak:** framework names without risk/control/evidence reasoning.
