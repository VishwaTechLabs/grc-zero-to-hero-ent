# 📚 Detailed Teaching Pack — Iso 27002

## 1. Why it exists
Teach the business problem the framework/standard is intended to help address.

## 2. Scope
Explain which organizations, processes, systems or use cases may be relevant.

## 3. GRC implementation pattern
**Scope → Applicability → Risk → Requirement/Outcome → Control → Evidence → Test → Finding → Improvement**

## 4. Classroom exercise
Select five requirements/outcomes. For each, document:
- requirement/outcome
- business risk
- internal control
- owner
- evidence
- test
- finding/remediation if applicable

## 5. Model answer
A valid mapping must be specific enough to explain why the internal control addresses the
requirement/outcome and what evidence would demonstrate implementation.

## 6. Common misconception
A framework mapping is not automatically a certification, audit opinion or legal conclusion.
